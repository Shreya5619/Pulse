# User Profile Metadata

## Persona
The user is a "Dynamic Commuter" — someone whose schedule is dense and involves mid-range travel between home, campus, and work.

## Key Constraints
- **Transit Dependent**: Relies on a mix of metro and ride-hailing.
- **Battery Anxious**: Very concerned about phone dying during transit (threshold: 20%).
- **Time Sensitive**: Punctuality is critical for labs and meetings; less critical for study groups.

## Trusted Channels
- Primary: Mobile App Push.
- Secondary: SMS (only for critical delays).
